## Section 5: Outreach Sequence

### Wave 1 — Days 1–3

**Who:** Adams (UF), Soto (UF), Antonenko (UF) + FGCU math ed / elementary ed faculty (to be identified)
**Send:** Personalized versions of Template A (Adams, Antonenko) and Template B (Soto). For FGCU: use Template B adapted — open with "I used to work at FGCU in the Agribusiness department" before the pitch. This is your warmest cold email.
**Goal:** Get at least one call scheduled with a senior UF contact; get at least one FGCU response (former employee connection means higher response rate here)
**Note:** Send on Tuesday, Wednesday, or Thursday morning — avoid Monday morning and Friday. Before emailing FGCU cold, check if you have any former colleagues still on campus who can make an introduction first.

### Wait — Days 4–10

Do not follow up within the first week. Faculty read email but schedule is compressed. Sending too fast signals anxiety, not confidence.

### Wave 2 — Days 11–14 (if no response from Wave 1)

**Who:** Velasco (UF) — new contact, not a follow-up
**Send:** Template A adapted for peer/co-authorship tone
**Also:** Send first follow-up to any Wave 1 contacts who have not responded

**Follow-up format for Wave 1 non-responses:**
> Subject: Re: [original subject line]
> [First name], following up briefly on the note I sent [X] days ago. Happy to send a one-pager or a short product brief if that would make it easier to evaluate whether there's a fit. Otherwise, a 20-minute call works — I'm flexible.

One follow-up per contact. Do not send a third email unless they responded to the second.

### Wave 3 — Days 21–28

**Who:** Apraiz, Beckett, Burklew (UF) + identified UM math ed contacts + FGCU contacts
**Send:** Template B for clinical faculty; Template A adapted for UM/FGCU math ed faculty
**Also:** Send placement coordinator emails (Template C) to UF, UM, and FGCU simultaneously — these are operational contacts and volume helps

### Phone / LinkedIn Escalation — Day 35+

If a high-priority contact (Adams, Soto, Antonenko) has not responded to email + one follow-up:

1. **LinkedIn first** — connect with a short note referencing your email. Do not paste the full pitch. Say: "Sent you a note about a classroom research collaboration — not sure if it landed. Happy to connect here if easier."
2. **Phone call** — only if LinkedIn produces no response AND the contact is genuinely high-value. Call the COE main number, ask to be connected to their assistant or leave a voicemail. Keep it to 30 seconds: name, what you built, why you're calling, your callback number.
3. **Conference networking** — If you're attending NCTM, AMTE, or SITE, these are the warmest possible outreach moments. Email in advance to schedule a 10-minute hallway conversation.

### Success Criteria by 60 Days

- At least 1 call completed with a UF math ed faculty member
- At least 1 placement coordinator has received Template C and responded
- At least 1 UM or FGCU contact identified and emailed
- Working toward: one research collaboration agreement (even informal) and one approved placement site
